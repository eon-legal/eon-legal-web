# Theme: Monochrome Elegance

## Overview
This theme, "Monochrome Elegance," is designed for a sophisticated and clean aesthetic. It leverages a strictly monochrome palette with a focus on typography and subtle detailing to convey professionalism and clarity. The design prioritizes content readability and a refined user experience.

## Color Palette
The primary color mode for this theme is **light**.

### Primary Brand Colors
*   **Primary Color:** `#000000` (Black) - The dominant color, used for key actions, headlines, and primary interactive elements.
*   **Secondary Color:** `#708090` (Slate Gray) - A supporting gray for less prominent UI elements, secondary actions, and subtle accents.
*   **Neutral Color:** `#FFFFFF` (White) - The foundational background color, ensuring maximum contrast and a clean canvas.

## Typography
The typography strategy combines a classic serif for headlines with a modern sans-serif for body and labels, striking a balance between traditional elegance and contemporary readability.

*   **Headline Font:** `Poppins` - Providing gravitas and sophistication to headings.
*   **Body Font:** `Inter` - A highly readable and versatile sans-serif font, ideal for long-form content and general UI text.
*   **Label Font:** `Inter` - Consistent with the body font to maintain visual harmony and ensure legibility for interactive labels.

## Shape and Form
The design embraces a subtle level of roundedness to provide a soft yet structured appearance, avoiding overly sharp or completely pill-shaped elements.

*   **Roundedness:** `1` (Subtle roundedness) - Corners of UI elements have a slight curve, offering a friendly touch without losing professionalism.

## Spacing and Layout
A normal, balanced approach to spacing ensures readability and visual comfort without making the interface feel too dense or excessively sparse.

*   **Spacing:** `2` (Normal) - Provides adequate whitespace between elements, contributing to a clean layout and comfortable user experience.
